Xlsx/PivotStyles.xml.gz holds the definitions of Excel's built-in pivot table styles (the predefined table styles of
ECMA-376 Part 1) as Apache POI ships them in presetTableStyles.xml (poi-ooxml, commit
f66927954a29cf1f5b86614fdf1816e6321c8aea), reduced to the pivot table styles by tools/generate_pivot_styles.py.
